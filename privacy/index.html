Who we are

This website, onjaal.com, is operated by Onjaal (“Onjaal”, “we”, “us”). Onjaal is responsible for the personal data described in this policy. For any question about this policy or your personal data, email tech@onjaal.com.

What this policy covers

This policy explains how we handle personal data collected through this website, including the enquiry form and emails you send us. It does not cover data that our customers store in Onjaal HRMS as part of using the product; that is governed by the agreement with each customer.

Information we collect

We collect only what you choose to give us, plus limited technical data needed to deliver the website.

  • Enquiry form: your full name, work email, company, team size and, if you provide them, your phone number, area of interest and message.
  • Emails: the contents of any email you send us and your email address.
  • Technical data: our hosting provider may process technical information such as your IP address and browser details to deliver and secure the website.

We do not use analytics, advertising trackers or social media pixels, and this website does not set its own cookies. If you choose a light or dark theme, that choice is stored in your browser’s local storage on your own device and is never sent to us. The interactive Onjaal HRMS preview runs entirely in your browser on fictional data; nothing you do in it is collected or sent to us.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and arrange a demo or conversation.
  • To prepare a proposal if you ask for one.
  • To keep a record of our business communication with you.
  • To inform you about upcoming products if you register early interest.

We do not sell your personal data or use it for automated decision-making.

Your consent

We process the personal data you submit on the basis of your consent, given when you send the enquiry form or email us, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. You can withdraw your consent at any time by emailing us. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that happened before you withdrew it.

Who we share it with

We share personal data only with service providers that help us run this website and respond to you:

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which delivers enquiry form submissions to our email inbox.
  • Our email service provider, which stores the emails we send and receive.
  • GitHub, which hosts this website through GitHub Pages.

These providers may process data outside India. We may also disclose information where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry information only as long as needed for the purposes above, or as required by law. You can ask us to delete it sooner.

Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the right to:

  • obtain a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we process it;
  • have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected, completed or updated;
  • have your data erased, unless we must keep it by law;
  • withdraw your consent;
  • have your grievances addressed; and
  • nominate another person to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity.

To exercise any of these rights, email tech@onjaal.com. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect personal data, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Please don’t include sensitive employee or personal data in the enquiry form.

Children

This website is intended for businesses and is not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Contact and grievances

For questions, requests or grievances about your personal data, email tech@onjaal.com.
