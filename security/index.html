HR data is personal. Here is exactly what this website does, what the product preview does, and what we will put in writing before you commit.

This website

What onjaal.com does, and does not do.

No tracking

We do not use analytics, advertising trackers or social media pixels. This website does not set its own cookies.

Your theme choice stays on your device

If you pick light or dark mode, that choice is saved in your own browser only. It is never sent to us.

Fonts and files are self-hosted

The site’s fonts, styles and scripts come from onjaal.com itself, not from third-party font or script networks.

The enquiry form

When you submit the contact form, your details are delivered by email to tech@onjaal.com through a form-handling service. See the privacy policy for what we keep and why.

The interactive preview

Try it without handing over anything.

The Onjaal HRMS preview on the home page is a conceptual demo built for this website. It uses fictional people and figures, runs entirely in your browser, makes no network requests and stores nothing. Closing the tab clears it.

Before you commit

Questions we will answer in writing.

For your deployment, we confirm the following in your written proposal. If your team needs a security questionnaire completed, ask us. Security review support is part of the Enterprise plan.

  • Where your data is hosted
  • Who can access it, and how access is controlled
  • How data is protected in transit and at rest
  • How backups and recovery are handled
  • How you can export your data
  • What happens to your data if you leave
  • How we handle and report incidents
  • Which sub-processors are involved

We do not publish claims we cannot back with documents. If something is not in place for your deployment, we will tell you so.

A useful checklist

Ask any HR software vendor these.

Use it on us, and on everyone else. A good vendor will answer plainly.

  1. Who can see employee records inside your own team? Look for role-based access, so managers see their team and HR sees more.
  2. Is there an audit trail? You should be able to tell who changed what, and when.
  3. Can we get all our data out? In a usable format, without a fee or a fight.
  4. What is your recovery plan? Backups are only useful if restores are tested.
  5. Who else touches our data? Ask for the list of sub-processors.
