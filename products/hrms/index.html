Frequently asked questions

Can’t find your answer? Email tech@onjaal.com.

Is the interactive preview the real Onjaal HRMS?

No. It’s a conceptual preview built for this website with fictional people and figures, so you can get a feel for how employee records, attendance, leave and approvals fit together. Nothing you do in it is saved or sent. Request a demo to see the live product.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. Teams of up to 5 employees can use Onjaal HRMS on the Free plan at ₹0. Paid plans start at ₹59 per user per month on annual billing.

How much does Onjaal HRMS cost?

Free for up to 5 employees. Standard is ₹59 and Premium is ₹99 per user per month on annual billing (₹71 and ₹119 billed monthly), excluding applicable taxes. Enterprise is custom. See the pricing page for the full comparison and an estimator.

Will it work with our biometric attendance device?

Biometric integration is ₹15,000 one-time on Standard and included on Premium. Whether it’s possible depends on your device and its API compatibility, so the scope is confirmed before any commitment. Mentioning your device make and model in your enquiry helps.

Does Onjaal HRMS handle payroll and statutory compliance?

Onjaal HRMS supports payroll-related HR workflows. The scope of statutory calculations and compliance is confirmed during your demo, so you know exactly what is covered for your organisation.

What does guided implementation include?

It’s an optional one-time service from ₹60,000 for a 150-user deployment: initial setup and configuration, aligning workflows and requirements with your team, data preparation guidance, and user onboarding and handover. Other team sizes are scoped in your proposal.

What happens after we send an enquiry?

Our team will review your enquiry and get back to you. The details you share help us prepare a more relevant conversation and demo.
