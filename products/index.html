Why a connected suite

Fewer tools. One shared picture.

Growing businesses often run people, customers, purchasing and accounts in separate tools. The Onjaal suite is designed so each product shares the same foundations, starting with your people in Onjaal HRMS.

  • One brand, one design language and one way of working across products
  • Guided implementation instead of a do-it-yourself setup
  • Pricing published upfront, starting with Onjaal HRMS

Frequently asked questions

Can’t find your answer? Email tech@onjaal.com.

When will Onjaal CRM, ERP and Accounts be available?

They are in development and no release dates have been announced. Register early interest through the contact form and we’ll keep you informed.

Is the interactive preview the real Onjaal HRMS?

No. It’s a conceptual preview built for this website with fictional people and figures, so you can get a feel for how employee records, attendance, leave and approvals fit together. Nothing you do in it is saved or sent. Request a demo to see the live product.

What happens after we send an enquiry?

Our team will review your enquiry and get back to you. The details you share help us prepare a more relevant conversation and demo.
