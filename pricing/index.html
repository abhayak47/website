Pricing questions

Can’t find your answer? Email tech@onjaal.com.

Is the Free plan really free?

Yes. Teams of up to 5 employees can use Onjaal HRMS on the Free plan at ₹0. No card is required. Request access and our team will set you up.

What happens when we grow past 5 employees?

You move to Standard, billed per user. We will tell you before that happens and confirm the change in writing, so there are no surprises.

What is the difference between Standard and Premium?

Standard covers the full HR workspace: records, attendance, leave, approvals, documents and standard reports. Premium adds biometric attendance integration, advanced reporting and priority support.

How does annual billing work?

Annual plans are billed once a year at ₹59 (Standard) or ₹99 (Premium) per user per month. Billed monthly, the rates are ₹71 and ₹119. Annual billing saves about 17%.

What does guided implementation include?

It is an optional one-time service from ₹60,000 for a 150-user deployment: initial setup and configuration, workflow and requirements alignment, data preparation guidance, and user onboarding and handover. Smaller or larger teams are scoped in your proposal.

Will it work with our biometric attendance device?

Biometric integration is ₹15,000 one-time on Standard and included on Premium. Whether it is possible depends on your device and its API compatibility, so scope is confirmed before any commitment. Mentioning your device make and model in your enquiry helps.

Are taxes included?

No. Prices are in Indian rupees and exclude applicable taxes, which are confirmed in your written proposal.

What should a larger organisation choose?

Enterprise is scoped around your workflows, integrations, rollout and contract. We recommend it from roughly 200 users, or sooner if you need custom integrations. Send an enquiry and we will prepare a proposal.

What happens after we send an enquiry?

Our team will review your enquiry and get back to you. The details you share help us prepare a more relevant conversation and demo.
