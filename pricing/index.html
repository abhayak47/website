Pricing questions

Can’t find your answer? Email tech@onjaal.com.

What does the annual subscription include?

The ₹1,06,200 annual subscription covers the Onjaal HRMS workspace for 150 users, which works out to ₹59 per user per month. It includes core HR workflows: employee and leave management, attendance and approvals, and HR documentation and reporting.

What is covered by the ₹60,000 implementation fee?

It’s a one-time starting scope: initial setup and configuration, aligning workflows and requirements with your team, data preparation guidance, and user onboarding and handover.

Our team isn’t exactly 150 people. Can we still enquire?

Yes. The pricing shown is indicative for a 150-user deployment. Share your team size in the enquiry form, and final scope and commercial terms will be confirmed in a written proposal.

Will it work with our biometric attendance device?

Biometric integration is optional and costs ₹15,000 one-time. Whether it’s possible depends on your device and its API compatibility, so the scope is confirmed before any commitment. Mentioning your device make and model in your enquiry helps.

What do we pay after the first year?

From year two, the subscription is ₹1,06,200 per year, subject to renewal terms. Implementation and biometric integration are one-time charges.

What happens after we send an enquiry?

Our team will review your enquiry and get back to you. The details you share help us prepare a more relevant conversation and demo.
