An attendance policy checklist for Indian SMEs

Attendance disputes are usually policy gaps in disguise. Settle these points once and write them down.

Policy · 4 min read

Working hours and shifts

  • Define standard hours, shifts and weekly offs.
  • Say how half-days and short days are counted.

Grace and late marks

  • Set a grace period and say how many late arrivals are tolerated.
  • State what happens after that, and make it consistent.

How attendance is captured

  • Choose the method: biometric device, app, or manual entry.
  • Say what happens when the device is down or someone forgets to punch.

Regularisation

  • Define how employees correct a missed or wrong entry.
  • Set a window and an approver, so corrections do not pile up at month end.

Remote and field work

  • Say who may work remotely and how their attendance is recorded.
  • Define how on-duty and field visits are logged.

Overtime and holidays

  • State whether overtime is allowed, who approves it, and how it is recorded.
  • Publish the holiday calendar early in the year.

Keep it connected to leave

  • Leave, holidays and attendance should tell one story.
  • When approved leave updates attendance automatically, the monthly reconciliation shrinks.
