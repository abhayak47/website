Choosing HR software: 10 questions to ask

Most HR software looks the same in a demo. These questions show you the differences that matter after the contract is signed.

Buying guide · 5 min read

About fit

  • Does it handle the way we actually work, or will we bend our process to it?
  • Can employees and managers use it without training sessions?
  • Does leave flow into attendance without re-entry?

About cost

  • Is the pricing published? Hidden pricing usually means variable pricing.
  • What is recurring, and what is one-time? Ask for them separately.
  • What happens to the price when we add ten people?
  • Are taxes included or extra?

About rollout

  • Who does the setup, and what exactly is included?
  • How is our existing data brought in?

About the long term

  • Can we export all our data in a usable format?
  • How does support work, and who answers when something breaks?
  • What else is the vendor building, and will it connect to what we already use?

This guide is general information, not legal advice. Rules differ by state and organisation, so have your final policy reviewed by a qualified advisor.
